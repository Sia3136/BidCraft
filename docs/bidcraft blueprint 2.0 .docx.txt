BidCraft Blueprint 2.0

Trust-First Creator Marketplace



Vision

BidCraft is a creator-focused marketplace where artists, students, collectors, crafters, and hobby sellers can sell products through:

Auctions

Buy Now

Make Offer

while providing buyers with stronger trust mechanisms than traditional social-media-based selling.

The core philosophy of BidCraft is:

"Trust the product, not just the seller."
